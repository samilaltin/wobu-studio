This page explains what Mochi's Hot Spring does with information. We keep it short because the game collects very little itself.

## What stays on your device

Mochi's Hot Spring saves your progress (coins, baths, friends, settings and language) in a file on your device. We do not receive this file. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, it is gone. There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud save.

## What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location. We do not run our own server for the game, and it has no analytics service of its own. The advertising SDK described below reports some usage and diagnostic information to Google; your IP address can be used to estimate your general location.

## Ads and the advertising SDK

Mochi's Hot Spring shows optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch a short video to get a bonus. We never show an ad you did not ask for.

Ads come from Google AdMob, which is run by Google, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (version 24.9.0 in this release). Google publishes a list of what this SDK collects and shares; we describe it here from that list, which Google keeps for its newest SDK version, so details for the version in this game may differ slightly. To show and measure ads, the SDK may collect and share, over an encrypted connection, your IP address, how you interact with the app and ads, diagnostic information such as launch time and hang rate, and device identifiers, including your Android advertising ID and an app set ID. Google lists the purposes as advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

Because of this the game asks Android for the advertising ID permission and a few related ad-services permissions, and a network connection. It also keeps a short wake-up permission and a foreground-service permission that the ad library brings in.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on Android: Settings, Google, Ads). If you delete it, apps get a string of zeros instead.

The ads we request are limited to the mildest content level (Google's "G" level, suitable for all ages).

We do not control how Google handles this information. Google's own policy applies; it is described on Google's page "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services", which you can reach from Google's Privacy Policy.

## Consent in Europe and some other regions

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game checks at each launch whether Google's consent message has to be shown, and asks for ads only after that check allows it. Once a choice has been recorded, you can reopen it in Settings under "Privacy options".

## Children

Mochi's Hot Spring is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. The ad content level is kept mild because younger children may still play. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us at the address above and we will help remove it.

## How long information is kept

We keep nothing on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete. Information held by Google as the ad company is kept under its policies; use its tools (for example resetting the advertising ID) to control it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data (for example access, correction and deletion under EU or UK law, or under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law). Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing to access. For data held by Google, contact Google. You may also write to us at the contact above.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for larger changes, tell you in the game.
